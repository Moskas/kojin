An Aliexpress prebuilt with flashed Vial firmware. With a couple of 3d printed homing keycaps as I kept loosing myself in relation to where my fingers where.  
~~Sadly some keycaps have already broken off at the stems so I need to get a new ones.~~
Replaced the original keycaps with LDSA keycaps and changed most of the switches over to sunset chocs.

| spec    | value                 |
|---------|-----------------------|
| Switch  | Kailh Choc Sunsets v1 |
| Keycaps | LDSA Keycaps          |
