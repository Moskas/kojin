I've been a fan of the Backrooms as a concept for years, both through YouTube video series and games. So when Kane Parsons hinted in his YouTube series that a movie was in the works, I knew I had to see it.  
The movie didn't disappoint. The atmosphere and set design were on point. At first, I was afraid it would focus too much on the actors rather than the Backrooms themselves, but the characters were there to serve the setting and the narrative, not to be the main focus of the movie.  
For a Backrooms movie, it did a good job with the mystery and added some nice new details to Kane Pixels' series.
