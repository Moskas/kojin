A book that is a log of a **51 day** passage from Japan (Shimoda) to Canada (Victoria). The book is written by Rekka Bellum and Devine Lu Linvega aka [Hundred Rabbits](https://100r.co/). It's a great book portraying emotions and daily life on board of a small ship in a very harsh environment. I was fascinated by their creations for quite a while now so this book was a must read for me.
