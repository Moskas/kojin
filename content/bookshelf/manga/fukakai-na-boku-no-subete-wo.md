This is a manga that focuses on Mogumo, a non-binary person who starts working at a café for “crossdressing boys,” but soon finds out there’s more to it than that.  
In general, the manga explores feelings and thoughts about gender and how people can interpret the world around them.  
Other than that, it’s your typical romcom, but this one sucked me in right from the first chapter, and I ended up reading the whole thing within 24 hours of starting it. Can recommend.
