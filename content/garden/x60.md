# Thermal paste replacement
In order to replace thermal paste you need to basically disassemble the whole bottom part of the laptop. Thankfully the screen and it's hinges aren't part of things to disassemble.
As it is with older ThinkPads you need to unscrew the keyboard first then you can unplug it from the top and then you can proceed to unscrew every screw from the bottom. The only thing you can omit is the ram cover as there are no screws underneath it.
After that you need to unplug the palmwrests fingerprint scanner if you have one and then lift the top cover. Then it's a matter of unscrewing a couple of screws from the metal brackets over drive sata connector and lcd connector. Don't miss the screws that are partially hidden in expansion card cage (I didn't see them at first and it was a bit frustrating as the heatsink is screwed to it as well)
Finally you can lift and turn over the mainboard and unscrew the rest of the heatsink screws.
## tl;dr
1. Keyboard screws and remove the keyboard
2. Palm rest and bottom cover screws, remove palm rest
3. Unscrew sata connector shield, express card screws and lcd connector 
4. Lift the pcb and unscrew the heatsink
5. Do everything in reverse
